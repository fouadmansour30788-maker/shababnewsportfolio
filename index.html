04 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE VISION

Meet
Firas Mawlawi.

A career shaped by communication, creativity and strategic vision.
Founder & CEO · Shabab News / Adiology

With an academic background in Political Science and Business Administration, Firas founded Shabab News in 2001 as an educational magazine designed to inform, engage and create meaningful impact.

In 2008, he expanded the brand into an advertising agency. His work has since spanned branding, social media advertising, video production and corporate events for ministries, hospitals, syndicates, universities and other institutions.

As the agency’s scope grew, he led its evolution into Adiology, a contemporary creative identity grounded in more than two decades of experience.
